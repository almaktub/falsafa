fal·sa·fa FAL-sə-fə فلسفه
noun, Arabic (from Greek philosophia). Philosophy, as it was carried, translated, and pushed further by the Islamic world for roughly five hundred years while a chunk of Europe wasn't reading much of anything. this site. Big ideas, explained the way I'd explain them to you at a kitchen table, not a lecture hall.

I picked this word because most philosophy sites and syllabi skip a five-hundred-year stretch of the story like it didn't happen, and I got tired of it happening quietly. This isn't a survey course. It's the stuff that actually rearranged something in me, told the way I'd tell it to you in person: no robes, no incense, no pretending the big names didn't have bad days, bad takes, and one or two genuinely unhinged decisions.

Big words, explained like a person is talking to you.

Philosophers you've heard of and a few you should have. No lecture hall tone, no boring big words thrown around to sound smart. Just the ideas that are actually worth carrying around, told straight.
